Introducing Little Things

Little nudges, with care

A simple, thoughtful reminder app designed to help you follow through on what matters most, exactly when it feels the most natural.

What is Little Things?

In its simplest terms, this is a reminder app. But traditional alarms are set to trigger at a specific, rigid time and day. While that's great for taking the trash to the curb, a daily reminder text to your partner at 10:00 AM on the dot eventually starts to feel a bit robotic. People want to feel thought of throughout the day, not just acknowledged on a schedule.

Little Things helps you configure gentle, randomized nudges within specific windows. Whether you want to spontaneous text your spouse, pause for prayer, or check in on a personal goal—the app takes care of the timing so your natural interactions can thrive.

Stay Connected & Present

Life happens in between your appointments. Customize your natural windows, let the timing be spontaneous, and ensure you make time for the small things.
