Little Things

Life Doesn’t Happen on a Schedule

Intentional, randomized reminders that help you stay present, nurture relationships, and follow through on what matters most.

Why Little Things Exists

Everyone says, “It’s the little things in life that mean the most.” Even with the best intentions, busy days have a way of keeping us preoccupied until the moment has already passed.

Traditional reminder apps work great for taking the trash to the curb at 7:00 PM. But real human connection isn’t meant to feel like an alarm clock. Little Things nudges you at spontaneous, natural moments within the windows you choose—so your thoughtfulness feels genuine, never robotic.

How It Works
Prompt • Occurrences • Per Interval • Days of Week • Time Window

Example: “Text my wife” — 1 time per day on M, T, W, Th, F between 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM. You’ll receive a spontaneous prompt once each weekday at an unpredictable moment.

Made for the Moments That Matter

Marriage & Relationships

Make Someone Feel Thought Of—Not Scheduled

A daily text at 10:00 AM on the dot quickly starts feeling automatic. A spontaneous note in the middle of an ordinary afternoon tells your spouse they were genuinely on your mind.

Family & Parenting

Capture the Mundane Before Bedtime Arrives

Between cooking dinner and folding laundry, entire days slip by in a blur. A once-a-week nudge to pause, step into the moment with your kids, or record a quick video gives you keepsakes from everyday life—not just vacations.

Faith & Gratitude

Pause for Prayer and Thankfulness

Build a deeper rhythm of faith throughout the noise of the day. Let unexpected moments prompt you to stop, say a quick prayer, and find something to be thankful for—no matter what’s happening around you.

Turn Good Intentions Into Everyday Habits

Stop letting busyness crowd out the things you care about most. Set your window, let the timing stay human, and show up for the little things.
