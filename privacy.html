Overview

Little Things ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. In the free version, all reminder titles and settings are stored anonymously as guest sessions with no personally identifying information collected. In the paid version, you may create an account and sync calendar permissions strictly to schedule reminders around your events.

Information We Collect

Reminder Titles: Stored securely and synced if using the paid cloud account.

Calendar Data: Accessed with your permission solely in-memory to coordinate reminders with your schedule. We do not store or sell your calendar details.
