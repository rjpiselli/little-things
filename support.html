Frequently Asked Questions

Does your app collect or share my information?

No. We store all data you enter into the app annonymously under guest sessions. That data is never analyzed, scraped, or shared.

My reminders are gone. Can they be restored?

No. All data is stored exclusively under annonymous guest sessions. If you clear app data, uninstall the app, reintall the app, switch devices, or in any other way cause data to be removed from your device - it is permanently lost. We cannot recover any data.

What does the Plus subscription add?

Plus adds access to 3 advanced features: 1. Calendar Considerations: the app will read your calendar to know when you have events scheduled, and then will use that information to schedule your reminders around those times. 2. Moments Journal: your completed reminders form the moments of your life. These are stored in a Moments Journal for you to access and reminisce on the beautiful memories you've created. You can also add notes to each Moment so you never forget the little details. 3. VIPs: these are the important people in your life. Adding a VIP allows you to link these people to Reminders, which links them to your Moments, allowing you to filter your journal entries by that special someone.

Why isn't anything being scheduled today?

If you're a Plus subscriber, go to the Plus membership page and turn off calendar considerations. If the reminder suddenly appears on your Today screen, you have a calendar event blocking it.
